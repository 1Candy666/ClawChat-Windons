# Scientific Paper Figures

## Scope and style

Use the research question and data type, not an assumed discipline. Follow the user's language. Select an available deterministic plotting tool suited to the data; GIS/PyGMT is an optional map branch, not a dependency for ordinary charts. Biological, medical, engineering, computational, social and humanities figures retain their own units and evidence conventions. Qualitative matrices and argument diagrams must not convert themes or interpretations into invented numerical scores.

Identify the question, comparison and requested change. Inspect the current render, source and accepted project style. Reuse verified inputs; cosmetic edits need no literature reload, model rerun or workbook rebuild.

Carry confirmed styling forward within the project: colours, text roles, frames, ticks and legends. Reuse an existing preset or record a compact preset for multi-figure work; project choices are not universal defaults. Scale typography by final panel width, not DPI. Keep panel tags separate from coordinates, units and legends.

Resolve routine layout/readability issues within the requested scope without further permission. Check related elements; respect explicit narrow edits and leave unrelated panels alone. Choose clearer encoding when needed; clarify only consequential scientific or scope ambiguities.

## Evidence and loading

Use deterministic plots for real data. Preserve provenance, units, denominators, thresholds, transformations and uncertainty meaning; never invent geometry or precision. Display resolution is not source support.

Read only the needed reference, not the full set:
- New statistics, masks or scope: [data-scope.md](references/data-scope.md).
- Map geometry, environment or furniture: [maps-pygmt.md](references/maps-pygmt.md).
- Terrain or palette changes: [map-styling.md](references/map-styling.md).
- Chart choice, labels, layout or tables: [plot-design.md](references/plot-design.md).
- A needed implementation pattern: [style-recipes.md](references/style-recipes.md).
- Promotion, renumbering or synchronization: [iteration-sync.md](references/iteration-sync.md).

**Ticks and frames follow the current figure design.** Prefer uncluttered labelled ticks; minor ticks are appropriate when scientifically useful, such as logarithmic axes. Do not impose one personal map-frame convention across disciplines. Frames, grids and class dividers have distinct roles.

## Deliver

Reuse a working environment. Test a small representative render before an unfamiliar plotting/export path; inspect errors before retrying.

Render, inspect at final size, repair: is meaning clear, every mark readable, styling consistent, and the artifact current? Check units, frame alignment, arrow shafts and legend/connector collisions, not just text bounds. Verify numerical identity for visual edits and agreement of requested exports with the preview.

Preserve the accepted version; distinguish candidate, checked and accepted outputs. Update only authorized dependencies; captions, collections and workbench follow their synchronization rules. Keep handoff brief. Finish unlocked exports and identify outstanding files.
