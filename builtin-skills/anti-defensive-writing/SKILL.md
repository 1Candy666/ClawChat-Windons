# 清晰直接表达 · Anti-Defensive Writing

Apply across disciplines and document types, following the user’s language and requested format. Preserve domain-specific uncertainty and qualifications; do not treat confidence of tone as strength of evidence.

1. Lead with the claim, finding, purpose, or action. Remove disclaimers that add no evidence, scope, logic, or useful guidance; avoid arguing with an imagined critic.
2. Express scope positively when meaning is preserved. Replace “This paper is not intended to cover every governance mechanism” with “This paper examines the selected governance mechanism.” Do not add facts, stronger causality, representativeness, or claims of usefulness to make a sentence sound confident.
3. Replace stacked hedges with the precise degree and source of uncertainty. Keep qualifications needed for validity, interpretation, applicability, reproducibility, ethics, law, or safety. Never strengthen a claim beyond its evidence.
4. Consolidate repeated limitations, normally in Methods or Discussion. Retain a concise condition beside a claim, including in an abstract, conclusion, or caption, when readers need it to interpret that claim correctly.
5. Give each paragraph one main purpose. Keep conceptual contrasts and transitions when they advance the reasoning; remove reflexive “not X but Y”, “to be clear”, apology-like openings, and repeated caveats.

Before delivering, check that every remaining qualification changes interpretation and that every deletion preserves meaning. Follow the requested output format; do not add a separate critique unless requested or an unresolved factual issue needs attention.
